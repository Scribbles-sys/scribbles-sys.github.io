Pokémon Adventures Volume Two & Community

Life is not about finding the circumstance that makes you happiest, or being in the most productive position there is. Life is about serving Christ in the place where He has put you right now, and following Him throughout every place you are. Important to your place, perhaps even its defining feature, is the people who inhabit it. A church is a place you belong to. You commit yourself to a church when you obtain membership to it, and what Christ has brought together let no man separate. Many people in your church will be willing and able to come to your aid and be your companion. Many people in your church will require a great deal of work on your part to be your comrade. Many people in your church will be insufferable, perhaps even be falling from the grace of God. These are all your brothers and sisters in Christ and you must work with all of them evenly and seriously. [Ecclesiastes 4; Acts 2; Galatians 6:1-10; Colossians 3] Red is faced with a similar situation in Pokémon Adventures, and has to work with many different people, some scary, some annoying, some inspiring awe.

There is a moment where Misty saves Red from drowning at the hands of a Dragonite – it may be lost on the reader thirty years on and more, but at this point Dragonite is one of the most powerful creatures to exist in the world of Pokémon. Misty is watching Red after their training because she knows he needs to succeed. She also knows that Red is no match for a Dragonite, and alas, Red is pinned to the bottom of the sea floor by a large boulder in the attempt to grab the Hidden Machine that the Dragonite is hovering around. This machine has the ability to teach a Pokémon how to carry people across deep waters on its back, along with giving the Pokémon special battle abilities. This is an important item for Red's quest to be a better trainer; in fact, it's essential. Him going after it is not suspect, but nonetheless this place in the world is above his ablities. It is good, then, that he has people he can rely on, even if he doesn’t think about it: "Reckless as usual, I see. Going after an item in a place like this. . . If you wanted to cross the sea, you should have just come to Misty, the Water Pokémon expert!!"[1] Misty is bright and enthusiastic to be there, even happy, ethough it is a great burden for her – although it is not difficult to want to stop what you're doing and save someone from great bodily harm or death. The relationship they formed meant something and wasn't temporal. It wasn't just for feeling well about being connected with other people, neither. Misty was willing to use a lot of her limited time – "I'm a Gym Leader. I have a life, okay? I only followed you 'cause I figured you'd have trouble crossing the sea"[2] – to help Red on his journey, as Red was willing to help Misty in the previous volume. They formed a connection by doing that, one that exists in realms not visible to the eye though is shown through outward expressions. She also trades her Gyarados for Red's Krabby now that the Hidden Machine has been destroyed. This, of course, allows Red to traverse the seas, but it also is a permanent and powerful edition to his roster of Pokémon, and adds a difficult to obtain creature to his Pokédex.

Right after Misty's help, Red meets Team Rocket again, who are looking for Blaine. Blaine once worked for Team Rocket, desiring the control over himself and Pokémon that they do. However, after watching the team grow a Pokémon themselves in a vat, Blaine cannot square his consciousness for what he has witnessed, and as such defects. Their introduction is Blaine saving Red also – Team Rocket spots Red hanging off a cliff spying on them on the islands and decide “He’s seen us. That’s enough. Eliminate him,” and Blaine jumps out of hiding: "Wait!! Leave the boy alone. I'm the one you want, right?"[3] The two pair up to fight Team Rocket but are quickly overwhelmed when the latter brings out their Moltres, one of the most powerful Pokémon in the Kanto region – and, this early in the Pokémon franchise, one of the strongest Pokémon in general. Blaine notices the a rock that Giovanni gave Red and realizes it is a fossil and rushes Red off to have it worked on. "Up ahead… is my secret laboratory. This Rapidash will take you there! I know we just met, Red, but… please trust me…"[4] Blaine's rush leaves out all details and worries Red, but Blaine's Rapidash shows Red that Blaine has a machine that can turn fossils, like the rock Red obtained from Giovanni, into the Pokémon it was years ago, an Aerodactyl. This is important because it can fly and therefore attack Moltres on more even terms. Blaine holds a small and sudden place in Red's life, as do many of the people in the series, but he is still important, both at this moment and in moments in the future. Red wants to go down swinging, but Blaine uses his wisdom to make both of their escapes, as neither are in any position to take on something like Moltres. Blaine helps guide Red where he needs to go, sends him in the proper direction.

Red also meets Green. Green is a malicious thief. She robs Red of his money through her peddling scheme, and when she is caught up with and beat up, she robs Red of his badges. That would perhaps be the last of things, but coincidentally, Red decides to sneak into Team Rocket to see what exactly the secret society does, and they all happen to go after Green while he is there – Green has stolen sensitive data from the group, data that is important for a project they are working on. Knowing this, Red thinks to himself "All of a sudden I don't totally hate Green… but what's this 'Mew' they keep talking about?"[5] This "Mew" is a mythical Pokémon that Red and Blue and Green – and perhaps all of the trainers in Kanto – want to capture. It is the Pokémon that Blue was fighting when he and Red first met. Mew is extremely elusive, such that even with the advanced knowledge of the Pokédex, nothing is known about it aside from the fact that it's elusive and quite powerful. As they do in fact catch up with Green, Red jumps in and knocks Green out of the way of an attack, forfeiting his cover and joining her in the fight – though Green is clever, and capable of defending herself. The pair naturally team up to find Mew – in fact, Green has much better skills and technology for finding Mew than Red, so he is moreso tagging along with her. Of course, Green is not quite perfectly aligned with Red:

"So, why are you looking for Mew?"
"Isn't it obvious? For M-O-N-E-Y! ♥"[6]

And of course, they meet Team Rocket looking for Mew. As Team Rocket are powerful, fighting them and dealing with Mew proves too much, and Red makes the executive decision to free the Mew from them rather than try to fight them for the chance of catching it. Mew is grateful, flattening Team Rocket and sparing our duo, and dashes away. Green, of course, had left Red to suffer.

"Mwa-ha-ha! At least I'll make a few bucks off the first ever photos of the 'Phantom Pokémon'! ♥. . ."
"Don't tell me while I was fighting for my life, you were…"[7]

After this, she gives Red back his badges - except they're just fake badges. She keeps the real ones for herself, as will be revealed in Volume 3. So, to some extent, Green is an adversary – certainly not wholly aligned. She is nonetheless aligned. At the end of this volume, Green extends an invitation to Blue to help her make it into Saffron City, which has been enclosed with a psychic barrier. Blue, of course, has other ways of solving the problem, but this shows the camaraderie the three of them have established, albeit somewhat loose. It might be tempting to look at bickering and conflict as the cause of an issue between you and a brother and sister in Christ, but these things are merely indicators of a deep and inherent quality between you two. Perhaps it is sin separating you from Christ's body; perhaps it is the Holy Spirit directing your eyes towards our reward in Heaven and away from any idolization of our life within the Body. Red and Green never come along in this arc. They always fight. They still work towards and accomplish the same goal, defeating Team Rocket. There are many people you will not come along with in the Church. You will be appointed by Christ to be the villain for many of those who have been saved by the Holy Spirit. This is not an interruption by our sinful nature but the very method the Lord will use to bring all of His heirs and children into eternal life and everlasting salvation. We must trust in our perfect communion in Heaven, never letting ourselves take our eyes off of our real goal, that which our hearts truly desire, which is eternal communion with Christ, our Father, and the Holy Spirit. Christ died for our sins; you know this and yet are surprised when someone should disrespect the will of the Lord. Christ came to heal the sick, you and all of those around you, and He came to bind all of us together. The greatest way we can serve Him, therefore, is to admit ourselves into the one Body, the Church.

Even in our modern America, there are many ways to die here. Nonetheless, we should be willing to support our brothers and sisters in Christ regardless of the passion we feel for them or the urgency we sense in the moment. Without other people we are nothing. With no connections, we waste away. We were not created to be separated from other people. Support happens in every moment: in the moment you read this essay, the moments you spend after yard work laying down on your couch, the moment you spend in your car just before exiting and going into the grocery store, as you pause to watch clouds stroll by, when you wake up after a couple hours of fitful sleep. You will usually not have someone else there with you in moments like these, but connections are not being physically attached to someone but being attached emotionally, mentally, spiritually. Much like how Red, though often alone, is never solitary in any moment of his journey because of the connections he has made with Blue and Green and Blaine and Misty and others. They are there through their efforts and gifts and memories. The life of the Christian is like this. Many of us will be constantly surrounded by people, and many of us will be isolated, but all brothers and sisters in Christ are never solitary in their journey because they have their pastors and elders and deacons and their prayer groups and Bible study groups and book clubs and gardening clubs and hiking clubs and many other such things. We also need to recall the founder of all of these, the Lord Jesus Christ. He is the mother root of all of our connections, and we all would be horribly isolated and completely solitary without Him. Let us take communion with our Savior when we take communion with His body.
